Dinkey Dongle installation

Install
=======

setupdrv.exe


To Uninstall
============

setupdrv.exe /ufull

OR

Double click on ..
Uninstall_Win.vbs for Windows
Uninstall_Mac.sh  for Mac

*** IMPORTANT *** It is essential to RESTART the system after uninstalling.


To install quietly
==================

setupdrv.exe /q
